# Arena FPS

Prioritize responsive movement, readable enemy roles, weapon switching, resource
pressure and encounter rhythm. Design connected combat loops with verticality,
escape routes and clear attack telegraphs. Parameterize jump/dash/air control
according to the brief. Test movement while firing, recovery after damage/falls,
weapon interactions, encounter completion and readable hit/kill feedback.
Start with one arena, two weapons and two enemy roles unless the brief differs.
